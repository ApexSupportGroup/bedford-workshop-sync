Bedford Workshop Sync

An internal integration of Bedford Motors Pty Ltd

Bedford Workshop Sync connects the Google account of Bedford Motors, a vehicle repair workshop in Australia, to the workshop's own job management system. It reads the workshop's booking calendar and booking-related email so that bookings appear as jobs in the workshop system, and it keeps a calendar that the integration itself created up to date.

The integration is used only with Bedford Motors' own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It is operated for Bedford Motors by Apex Support Group, which builds and maintains the workshop system.
