Privacy policy

Bedford Workshop Sync · effective 19 November 2026

Who we are

Bedford Workshop Sync is an internal integration of Bedford Motors Pty Ltd ("Bedford Motors"), a vehicle repair workshop in Australia. It is operated for Bedford Motors by Apex Support Group, which builds and maintains the workshop's job management system. The integration is used only with Bedford Motors' own Google account and has no other users.

What Google data the integration accesses

  • Google Calendar (read only): events in the workshop's booking calendar.
  • Google Calendar (calendars the integration creates): the integration can create and update events only in calendars that it created itself. It cannot change any other calendar.
  • Gmail (read only): email messages, to recognise booking requests and booking notifications. The integration cannot send, change or delete email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to run Bedford Motors' own workshop operations: turning bookings into jobs in the workshop system, keeping a history of each vehicle's visits, and showing staff their schedule. The data is processed by software, including AI software, that runs on Bedford Motors' own server at its premises, to provide these workshop features.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

Bedford Workshop Sync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the data is kept and who can see it

Copies of calendar events and the job records made from them are stored on Bedford Motors' server at its premises. They are available to Bedford Motors staff through the workshop system, and to Apex Support Group engineers when they maintain that system on Bedford Motors' behalf. Access credentials are stored on that server with access restricted to the system account that runs the integration.

The data is not shared with any other party, except where required by law.

How long the data is kept

Booking and job records are kept as part of Bedford Motors' workshop records for as long as Bedford Motors needs them. If the integration is disconnected, synchronisation stops; Bedford Motors can ask for the copied calendar and email data to be deleted at the contact address below.

Disconnecting the integration

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Third-party connections page.
